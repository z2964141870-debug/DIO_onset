# Onset research protocol review

Status: exploratory hypothesis, not a demonstrated causal result or novel contribution.

The old comparison changed 30-second original windows into approximately 10-second middle windows. Start, content and exposure duration changed together. The reported fall reduction does NOT identify onset effects. Standing-only survival does not establish squat tracking. Previously reused clips are development data, not untouched heldout data. Three successful seeds out of four is an observed count, not a known population probability. Absence of search hits is not evidence of novelty. Two SONIC versions are not independent algorithm families.

Pilot: lock the same future reference frames, actual duration, control rate, preview, metrics and seed at each anchor. Compare conventional reset with a dynamic simulator checkpoint from a fixed preroll. Separate simulator state from policy history. Cross cold/warm history only when transplantation is meaningful and leakage-free; otherwise estimate a joint intervention only. Restore every arm independently. Do not fabricate history at frame zero or select only successful prerolls without reporting selection.

First validate checkpoint restore/replay equivalence. Primary endpoint is any fall in a fixed horizon; keep early failures. Estimate paired risk differences with session/subject-clustered uncertainty. Do not count frames or overlapping windows as independent. Use pilot to set a fixed sample size and effect threshold, then lock subject-disjoint heldout data before tuning.

Verify wearable IMU and GT provenance, alignment, rights and subject identities. FGP estimates are not GT. G1 checkpoint and motion loading is confirmed; closed-loop rollout is NOT yet verified. No new training or large sweep until those gates pass. Do not touch unrelated shared-server processes.
